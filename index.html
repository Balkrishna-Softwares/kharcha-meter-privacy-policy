Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure in Kharcha Meter?

Yes. Kharcha Meter is built on local-only database architecture. All your transaction records remain stored securely in the app database on your physical device. There is no cloud storage, no account signup, and no data tracking. We have no access to your financial logs.

What diagnostic data does the app collect?

To identify bugs and maintain app reliability, the App uses limited third-party services provided by Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics: Tracks anonymous features usage insights (e.g. screen transitions) to improve user flows. No transaction logs or values are ever sent.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Monitors software crashes to report diagnostic details (OS version, device model) to developers for patching.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Handles settings config like triggering mandatory app updates.
Why choose Kharcha Meter over cloud-connected trackers?

Kharcha Meter offers distinct advantages for users who value simplicity and data privacy:

  • Perfect for anyone who wants a lightweight, offline expense manager.
  • Helps you stay aware of your daily spending habits.
  • No ads, no distractions – just pure tracking made easy.
  • Designed with privacy and simplicity in mind.
Why does the app prompt for updates?

To ensure security compatibility, overall stability, and an improved loading experience, the app may occasionally prompt you to download mandatory updates from the Google Play Store.
